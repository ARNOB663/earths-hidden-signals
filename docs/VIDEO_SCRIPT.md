# 30-second demo video script

The rules allow at most **30 seconds** (or ≤ 7 slides). Anything longer is not eligible for global
judging, so aim for about 28 seconds. The voice-over below is about 70 words, roughly 2.5 words a
second at a calm pace. If you narrate in Bangla, add English subtitles. Keep NASA logos out of the
video.

Record the live site (https://earths-hidden-signals.vercel.app) in a 1920×1080 browser window in light
mode. Every number below is what the site shows today. Check it again just before recording, because
the data can be updated.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Home page: the headline, then scroll to the full-width warming stripes | "Earth's land is one and a half degrees warmer than in 1981." |
| 4–9 s | Climate trends, World: the red map; click one square so its number appears | "We tested every land area with NASA data. Almost all warmed — and we show where a change is real." |
| 9–15 s | Your place: type **Khulna**, the report opens on its stripes and "+1.4 °C" | "Type your town. Khulna: one point four degrees warmer, a clear change." |
| 15–20 s | Scroll to "By 2041–2060" (Almost every year) and "Recorded within 300 km" (51 flood alerts, 10 cyclones) | "By mid-century, today's hottest year comes almost every year. Fifty-one flood alerts and ten cyclones nearby." |
| 20–24 s | Click **Find its climate twin**: the side-by-side with South-eastern Brazil | "Its climate twin is in Brazil: same change, half a world away." |
| 24–28 s | Click **Download as image**, show the card; tap **বাং** to switch to Bangla | "Share it, in English or Bangla." |
| 28–30 s | End card: "Earth's Hidden Signals · earths-hidden-signals.vercel.app" | "Earth's Hidden Signals." |

## Tips
- Rehearse the clicks first, so the recording has no waiting for maps to load. The map pages are
  fastest on a second visit.
- Hide the mouse pointer between clicks, or use a big, clear cursor.
- The challenge text asks for "a couple of demonstration examples". The 2-minute tour on the home page
  is a second one, and you can mention it in the description, not the video.

## Backup: 7 slides
1. Title: Earth's Hidden Signals, with the global warming stripes.
2. The problem: climate data stops at a chart; people can't tell whether a change is real.
3. What we built: every land area tested (Mann–Kendall, Sen's slope, false-discovery control).
4. Your town: the Khulna report.
5. The future: NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6, six models, 2041–2060.
6. Honest science: the cross-check with CRU TS, and the AI test that found no skill.
7. Try it: the link, plus English and Bangla.
